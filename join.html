What you'll do

Students in the group analyze ATLAS data, help build detectors for the ATLAS upgrade and for P‑ONE, and learn large-scale computing along the way.

Analyze ATLAS data

Study proton collisions from the Large Hadron Collider.

Build detectors

Help build hardware for the ATLAS upgrade and for P‑ONE.

Compute at scale

Learn large-scale computing on the Tier-1 Centre and national supercomputers.

From first year

Undergraduate research

  1. First year: Adopt-a-Physicist

    First-year physics students can be "adopted" by a research group: join group meetings and gradually take part in the research. About Adopt-a-Physicist

  2. Summer research awards

    Undergraduate Student Research Awards (USRA) fund summer research. Canadian citizens and permanent residents apply for the NSERC award; international students for the SFU VPR Sciences award. About USRA at SFU Physics

  3. Ask about other positions

    Summer and co-op research positions are sometimes available. Email a faculty member to ask.
