High Performance Computing

The LHC produces enormous amounts of data, and analyzing and storing it takes powerful computing.

SFU hosts the ATLAS-Canada Tier-1 data centre, which processes and stores data from the ATLAS experiment, alongside national research supercomputing at SFU.

Students in the group learn to work at this scale, which prepares them for careers both in physics and in big-data computing.
